# Additional & Advanced Topics (Part 2 of Taylor)

Chapters 12–16 provide modern, advanced, and computational extensions to classical mechanics. Depending on the semester, instructors often cover Chapters 12–14 or assign them as computational term projects.

---

## Folders in this Section

* [ch12-chaos/](ch12-chaos/) — **Nonlinear Mechanics & Chaos**: Driven damped pendulum, period doubling, bifurcation diagrams, and state space.
* [ch13-hamiltonian/](ch13-hamiltonian/) — **Hamiltonian Mechanics**: Generalized coordinates and canonical momenta (`q, p`), phase space paths, and Liouville's theorem.
* [ch14-collision-theory/](ch14-collision-theory/) — **Collision Theory**: Differential cross sections (`dσ/dΩ`), impact parameter, and Rutherford scattering (CM vs. Lab frames).

---

## Other Part 2 Chapters (Reference)
* **Chapter 15: Special Relativity** (pp. 595–680): Four-vectors, Lorentz transformations, relativistic momentum and collisions.
* **Chapter 16: Continuum Mechanics** (pp. 681–738): Wave equations, stress and strain tensors, and fluids.
