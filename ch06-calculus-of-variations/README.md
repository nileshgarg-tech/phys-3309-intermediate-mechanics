# Chapter 6: Calculus of Variations

Optimization of path functionals and the foundation of analytical mechanics.

---

## Key Topics & Roadmap

* **The Variational Problem**:
  * Seeking the path `y(x)` between endpoints that makes the functional `S = ∫ f(x, y, y') dx` stationary (minimum or maximum).

* **The Euler-Lagrange Equation**:
  * `∂f/∂y - d/dx (∂f/∂y') = 0`

* **Classic Applications**:
  * **Shortest Distance**: Proving straight lines minimize arc length in Euclidean space.
  * **Fermat's Principle of Least Time**: Deriving Snell's law of refraction.
  * **Brachistochrone Problem**: Finding the curve of fastest descent under gravity (the cycloid).
  * **Surface of Revolution**: Minimal area catenary surface formed by soap films.

* **Systems with Multiple Dependent Variables**:
  * For multiple coordinates `y_i(x)`:
    * `∂f/∂y_i - d/dx (∂f/∂y_i') = 0` for each `i`.
